# Dash Punctuation
Spoken text from the video, recorded 2026-11-25.

Intro: Three different dashes. One key, three jobs.

1. Hyphen
Hyphen joins words. Example: well-known. Hyphen follows no fixed ratio

2. En dash
En dash marks a range. Example: 1990–2000. En dash is exactly half

3. Em dash
Em dash breaks a thought. Example: Design matters — maybe more. Em dash is one em wide

Closing: This is dash punctuation. Hyphen, en dash, em dash — three separate marks, three separate uses. Parsed, not guessed.
