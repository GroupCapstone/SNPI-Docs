# Easing Curves
Text from the video, recorded 2026-11-25.

1. Linear
Spinners, loaders, anything mechanical

2. Ease in
Things leaving the screen

3. Ease out
Things arriving on screen

4. Ease in-out
Moving things around the screen

5. Overshoot
Pop-ups and buttons with a bit of attitude

6. Elastic
Playful moments. Use sparingly

7. Bounce
Things that drop or land

8. Steps
Clocks, counters, stop-motion
