# 10 Niche Typography Hacks
Spoken text from the video, recorded 2026-11-25.

1. Reserved bold width
Reserve bold width on your text to prevent a layout shift on that row.

2. Text-box trim
Fonts leave room for descenders, which cause your text to be slightly off centre. Use text-box trim to fix this.

3. Underline offset
Default underlines get chopped up by every g and y. Use underline-offset to fix this.

4. Superscript spacing
The sup tag can create uneven spacing across your rows. Use line-height zero to fix this.

5. Fluid type
Instead of jumping heights at breakpoints, size your headlines with clamp so it scales with the screen.

6. Small caps acronyms
If acronyms in all caps come across too loud, use small caps instead.

7. Curly quotes
Straight quotes can come across as mechanical. Try using curly quotes instead.

8. Middle truncation
Don't truncate file names at the end because you'll lose the extension. Truncate in the middle.

9. Auto hyphenation
Narrow columns can create huge gaps between words. Turn on hyphens to let the browser split it for you.

10. Line-clamped titles
If your titles make your cards different heights, cap it with line-clamp.
