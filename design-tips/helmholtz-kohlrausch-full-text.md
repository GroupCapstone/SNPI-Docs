Helmholtz-Kohlrausch effect
Same measured luminance, yet they don't look equally bright. This is the Helmholtz-Kohlrausch effect. Saturated colors look brighter than they measure. Blue can look noticeably brighter than its measured luminance suggests. Yellow stays much closer to its measured luminance. And that's the catch. WCAG only measures luminance contrast. It doesn't account for how saturated colors are perceived. Equal on paper doesn't always look equal to the eye.
