# Goal-Gradient Effect
Spoken text from the video, recorded 2026-11-25.

Goal-gradient effect
Your stamp card works hardest right before it pays out. Right after the free cup, customers slow back down. So make every new card 12 spaces with 2 already stamped. Same 10 coffees, but the cup feels closer, so the card fills up faster. This is the goal-gradient effect. Effort speeds up as a reward gets closer.
