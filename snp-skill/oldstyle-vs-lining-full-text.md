# Oldstyle vs Lining Numerals
Text from the video, recorded 2026-11-25.

Numerals 3, 4 and 5 drop lower. Most software defaults to lining figures, locking every digit to cap height and interrupting rhythm. Oldstyle numerals let 3, 4, 5, 7 and 9 descend, 6 and 8 rise above the x-height, echoing natural ascenders and descenders.

Use oldstyle numerals for body text and footnotes. Keep lining numerals for tables, headlines and all-caps.
