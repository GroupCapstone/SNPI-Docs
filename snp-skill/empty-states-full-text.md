Empty states
An illustration alone makes your empty screen a dead end. So start with a headline that says what's missing. Then a short line adds why it's empty and what appears here. And one main button labeled verb plus noun gives the way out. But the tone depends on why it's empty. On first use, it motivates. After a cleared list, it celebrates. For an empty search, it stays neutral. And for an error, it stays factual. And none of them shows before loading ends.
