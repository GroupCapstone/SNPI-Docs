# 10 Niche Design Hacks
Spoken text from the video, recorded 2026-11-25.

1. Concentric radius
Rounded corners inside rounded corners shouldn't match. Make the inner the outer radius minus padding.

2. Alpha borders
Solid hex borders can look ugly depending on the background. Use alpha borders and they'll blend into whatever is behind them.

3. OKLCH palettes
Our eyes perceive brightness differently, so HSL isn't optimal. Use OKLCH instead.

4. OKLCH gradients
If your gradients look grey in the middle, add "in oklch" to stay vivid the whole way.

5. Matched icon weight
A thin icon looks sloppy beside bold text. Match the stroke to the font weight.

6. Masked edges
Fade out your carousels with a mask.

7. Blurred icon swaps
When icons swap in your app, add a little blur to make the transition more tasteful.

8. Dark mode depth
Shadows barely show in dark mode, so use lighter surfaces higher up to add depth.

9. Dynamic viewport
100vh can cause problems with Safari. Use dvh instead.

10. Multiply blending
If your photos have white backgrounds, use multiply for blending to make the white disappear.
