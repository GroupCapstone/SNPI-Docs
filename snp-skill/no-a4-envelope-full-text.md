# There Is No A4 Envelope (ISO 269)
Text from the video, recorded 2026-11-25.

There is no A4 envelope. A is the content: Letter, Notebook, Postcard. C is the envelope. And C4 fits A4 unfolded. B is the outer shell, sizing books and passports. C sits between A and B, the geometric mean of both. Fold A4 once, it fits C5. Twice, and it's C6.

A is Content, C is Envelope, B is Shell.
