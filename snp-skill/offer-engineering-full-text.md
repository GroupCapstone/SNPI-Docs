# Offer Engineering: How to Design "Irresistible" Offers
Text from the video, recorded 2026-11-25.

To design a great offer (course, coaching, service, etc), we need to first understand some basic psychology, namely that humans are at least twice as likely to move AWAY from something they don't want as they are to move towards something they do want. This means our offers will be more desirable if we can structure them in a way that shows the potential customer that our product or service will help them avoid unpleasant challenges or realities they would otherwise have to face.

To help people get what they want in an easier and/or faster way, we need to include at least 2 of the following 3 things:
1. "Done-for-You" elements that reduce the amount of action or effort required to get the desired outcome.
2. Systems and processes that provide clarity and reduce overwhelm, indecision, or inefficiencies.
3. Support of some kind to alleviate fear, self-doubt, and limiting beliefs that might prevent progress.

Result + pain relief: Focus 20% On the Desired Result and 80% On Removing the Pain of Getting There. People will always choose the path of least resistance, which is why making big promises can hurt you. The bigger the promise, the higher the perceived difficulty of getting the result.

Result (20%): Don't try to compete here. People can always make bigger and bigger promises. It doesn't take much creativity and doesn't make you stand out. People do pay for results, but you'll have an easier time promising smaller, more specific results than you will making larger promises.

Pain relief (80%): What's a bigger industry? Nutrition/Fitness or Pharmaceuticals? $30.8 BILLION / $1.48 TRILLION, about 50X bigger. People don't just pay for results, they pay for the SPEED and EASE at which they can get those results. They sell painkillers, not vitamins.

1. Action (d.f.y)
What's something they ordinarily have to do that you could either do FOR them or help make it much easier/faster to do it using pre-made resources? Examples: Pre-made website or funnel templates. Pre-made workout or meal plans. Pre-made planning worksheets or habit trackers. Fill-in-the-blank resources. Done-for-you service (full or partial).

2. Thinking (process)
How can you give them CLARITY on something that might otherwise be confusing? Can you create a streamlined step by step process that makes it so they don't have to THINK very hard about what to do and when to do it? Examples: Step by step training. Pre-made checklists or SOPs. Pre-determined daily/weekly/monthly routine. Customizable systems that can be adapted to their unique goals.

3. Fear (support)
How can you take away their fear and doubt? What kind of support can you provide? Can you "productize" that support? For example, creating pre-made resources that can help them with their mindset and beliefs. Examples: 1-to-1 or group coaching. Q&A or support group. Pre-made audio lessons to help with mindset and removing blocks to taking action (e.g. guided meditations). List of resources or frequently asked questions in case they get stuck.

Spoken summary: If you sell coaching or any type of online education, the biggest determining factor in how much money you're gonna make is gonna be how good your offer is. The big mistake people make when creating offers is they focus way too much on the result. Result is actually only like 20% of it. What people actually pay for is the pain relief element. When you remove the fear, when you remove the overthinking, and when you can remove as much action or effort as possible for somebody, that is what is gonna make them so much more likely to say yes to whatever you're selling.
