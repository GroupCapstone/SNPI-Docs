# App Rules to Not Get Sued
Spoken text from the video, recorded 2026-11-25.

Intro: Here's how to not get sued when building an app.

1. DMCA policy
If your users can upload or generate anything, this gives copyright owners a way to send you a takedown. Register a designated agent with the Copyright Office

2. EULA
Which basically sets the ownership rights for the app and says what users can and cannot do

3. Privacy policy
Because Apple and the Google Play Store require it, and it should also be posted on your website and inside of your app in HTML
