# 20 Principles of UX & Animation
Spoken text from the video, recorded 2026-11-25.

1. Squash & stretch
Things squish on impact to show weight

2. Anticipation wind-up
A small wind-up before the big move

3. Staging the focus
Direct the eye to one thing at a time

4. Pose to pose
Set the key poses, let the rest fill in

5. Follow through
Parts keep moving after the main body stops

6. Slow in & slow out
Speed up and slow down, never constant

7. Moving in arcs
Move along curves, not straight lines

8. Secondary action
A small flourish that backs up the main one

9. Snappy timing
Duration sets the feel. Keep UI under 300ms

10. Exaggerated feedback
Push it past real so the moment lands

11. Solid drawing in 3D
Give flat things depth and volume

12. Overall appeal
The sum of every detail, done with care

13. Fitts's law
Bigger, closer targets are faster to hit

14. Bigger hit areas
The clickable zone can be bigger than it looks

15. Hick's law
More choices, slower decisions, so chunk it

16. Miller's law
We hold about 7 things at once, so chunk it

17. Doherty threshold
Respond within 400ms or it feels slow

18. Postel's law
Accept messy input, give back clean output

19. Jakob's law
People expect your app to work like the others

20. Frequency of use
The more it's used, the less it should animate
