# Serial Position Effect
Spoken text from the video, recorded 2026-11-25.

Serial position effect
You remember the first and last items of a list best. How well you remember an item depends on its position. Early items get rehearsed more, while late items stay fresh. Thirty seconds of counting erased the last-item advantage. The first barely changed. Deciding right away: put the key option last. Deciding later: put it first. This is the serial position effect. Items at the start and end of a list are remembered best.
