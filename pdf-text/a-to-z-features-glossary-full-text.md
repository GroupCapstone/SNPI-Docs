## A. Admission System

Comprehensive admission form and various certificate formalities increase the carbon-footprints and create the vexing environment for the parents. Since sometimes, to complete all the formalities they have to miss their crucial working hours to complete the process.

With admission system, institutions can easily manage the student's record & data related to the students. Parents can easily attach any type of document online without visiting the school frequently. Adding to that, the system provides a biometric identification which saves time and effort of both the administrators and the parents.

### What all you can do

- Unique ID for all students.
- Comprehensive admission form.
- Multiple guardian addition facility.
- Emergency contact facility.
- Photo upload facility.
- Manage student records.
- Provide Biometric ID of the students.
- Previous education details can be recorded.
- Customizable as per school standards.
- Attach any type of document and data related to student.

## B. Bulk Data Management

An excel sheet has been a part of administrative system for a very long time. But when it comes to big data, institution require a proper data management system, to impeccably manage the valuable resource of the institution on a single platform.

Every institution has their own format of storing the data. With bulk data management system, administrators can easily categories the data and customize the fields according to the user demands. Most importantly, it provides data security along with recoverability using data backups.

### What all you can do

- Edit/Delete Data categories created.
- Manage entire data of the school.
- Customize the fields as required by the user.
- Robust data management system available.
- Export all your data from the system.

## C. Courses & Batches

Jumbled up course/batch structures are not conducive to a managed learning, and are quite troublesome to maintain. Courses & batches management system helps admin to keep a track of every old and new course and batches between a specified period.

This integrates well with other systems like admission, examination and many other, through which enrolling the students, defining the time table and creating the examination for every academic year becomes easy for the administrators for every student's specific batch.

### What all you can do

- Edit/Delete the course and batches.
- Assign subject fee amount for a batch.
- View the students in the batch.
- Assign tutor for a batch.
- Change/remove tutor for a batch.
- Import previous batch subjects and master fees while creating a new batch.
- If all the students are transferred to the new batch, the previous batch will automatically become deactivated or inactive.

## D. Dashboard Customization

"Oh sorry, it just slipped out of my mind", it's a very common phrase uttered by overloaded and stressed out people, when they are heavily indulged in their work and miss out on tasks. Here is when a flexible Dashboard comes handy.

A beautiful dashboard in your school structure which showcases various scheduled tasks, pending tasks, and notifies the user about various trends in their institution certainly makes your day. School ERP software facilitates customizable dashboard which gives an update on latest news, events, discussions, birthdays, and leave applications.

### What all you can do

- Dashlet driven dashboard.
- Great user experience with a user friendly interface.
- Anyone with basic computer knowledge can quickly use the system with minimum training.
- Innovative 'Search bar' enables anybody to use the School ERP System within seconds of login.
- Displays latest news, Events, discussions, birthdays, leave applications.

## E. Examination Management

Conducting exams is an arduous and time taking process. It takes months of preparation, countless paperwork and lots of laborious planning. With school software, institution can conduct an exams online and easily get a track on individual activities.

It's also easy to group the exams, create statistical and analytical report to track the student's progress. According to which, some measures can be taken by the teachers to improve their pupil's performance.

### What all you can do

- Create different types of exams based on grades & marks.
- Group exams if required.
- Online exams services with automatic marks calculation - Objective and descriptive exam types.
- Extensive report center.
- Generate report for required exams.
- Automated, quick and on demand report generation.
- Statistical and chart reports, a better analytical view.
- GPA, CCE, ICSE, Normal and CWA Evaluation methods.
- Setting up required formula for calculation in CCE, GPA and ICSE exams.
- ASL (Assessment for speaking and Learning) and 6th subject in CCE.
- CBSE prescribed report.
- Exam wise report, subject wise report, consolidated report, combined report and reports based on student rankings per class, batch, subject and attendance.

## F. Finance Management

Finance is a comprehensive and indispensable department of every institution. It handles the fees structure, payroll of employees, assets and liability of the institutions and many more. Handling it without any robust and reliable software is a daunting task for the finance department.

Finance system in school software provides a fast and efficient way to track each transaction and easily create a financial report, which can be used to get an insight on the profits and expenditures. Along with that, the staff can manage the online fee submission which shields the parents from unnecessary anxiety.

### What all you can do

- Comprehensive Fee classification system.
- Separate fee collection date designing system.
- Easy analysis of fee defaulters and fee structure available.
- Easy fee submission process.
- Fee import facility.
- Manage and Pay instant fees.
- Manage entire expenses and incomes.
- Customize and manage different category of expenses and incomes.
- Fee receipts printing settings.
- Financial Report generation facility with customizable date range.
- Payslip approval system.
- Manage Assets and Liabilities.
- Manage Donations.

## G. Gradebook

Gradebook is a way through which teachers keep a track on individual student's performance and maintain their grade records. But maintaining it everything on paper increases the workload, and shortens its lifespan to a few years at max.

With the gradebook in school ERP system, an administrator can create multiple examination structures based on the institution's education board such as CBSE, ICSE, etc.. Those structures can further be divided into one or more terms. Additionally, both subject based and non subject based evaluations can be created and marked.

### What all you can do

- Set up CEE/ICSE/CBSE grading system.
- Schedule exam for a batch and further divide into one and more terms.
- Set up GPA/CWA grading system.
- Set up the grading levels.
- Set up internal assessment calculation mode.
- Schedule an exam for multiple batches.
- Set up CCE grade sets for co-scholastic areas.
- Set up CCE weight-ages for each exam category.
- Set up Normal grading system for my institution.

## H. Hostel Management

Procuring a suitable roommate is a struggling task for each students. Along with it, the security and comfort of students is also a priority for the hostel management crew. Besides that, the vast tomes of student records and cumbersome paperwork is prone to human error.

Implementing this school ERP in an institution eases out the room allocation for warden, whereas the students can easily process a room change request. Besides that, a warden can keep all the records safely stored in an encrypted storage. Hostel keeper can also collect the hostel fee online.

### What all you can do

- Manage Hostel.
- Add/Edit/Delete/View different types of Hostel & Rooms.
- Allocate rooms to the students.
- Create hostel fee collection date.
- Manage hostel fee collections.
- View hostel fee defaulters.
- Pay hostel fee - student wise.
- Change rooms for students.

## I. Inventory Management

Handling vast tomes of inventory data is a daunting task for inventory managers. Manually tracking the item's order details and supplier information is not only time taking process but also prone to calculation error. An ERP can make things effortless.

With school ERP system, institutions can efficiently get a track on utilization of the materials or items, easily maintain and record the supplier details. Along with that manage the digital invoices and preserve the notes on particular items online.

### What all you can do

- Manage and create store categories, store types, store items, suppliers and supplier types.
- Create Indents.
- Manage Purchase order.
- Create GRN.

## J. Journey Management

Providing an excellent learning journey and fostering the personal growth and self esteem of each student is the vital part of every institution. But it becomes troublesome task for the teachers and admins when they do not have a proper system to track the individual performances.

With School ERP software, admins and teachers can monitor the student's growing and learning journey from their admission to the time they leave the school.

### What all you can do

- Get a track on their daily attendance.
- Can filter their task and download the document uploaded by faculty.
- Collaborate with students and other users via text, audio and/or video at anytime.
- Get instant notification about events, news, fee schedules, new forums and holidays etc.

- Students can do online submission of notes / assignments.
- Students can get a track on the entire placement activities.

With employee management system, without investing extra in manpower, institution can keep a record and organize all employee details in a single platform. Automates the loss of pay calculation and monthly/weekly payslip generation.

### What all you can do

- Manage entire payroll efficiently.

- Generate payslips on daily, weekly, monthly basis.

- Automatic Loss of Pay calculation.

## O. Online Assignment

Students find assignment submission tedious and boring. With online assignment management system, teachers can easily track the submission and allot the submission date so that everyone will remain aware of their duties. It is also eco-friendly, since it discourages paper wastage and with proper system the records can be maintained for a long time.

### What all you can do

Using the secure payment gateway, users like parents, teachers and admins can perform secure online fee payments by methods such as credit cards, debit cards, net banking, and so on. Using the school software, transactions are be handled securely through encrypted channels.

### What all you can do

- Account Administrator can configure and assign payment gateways to the institutions.

## U. User Management

This created problems with difficulty in reorganizing existing roles, creating new roles, and maintaining which role is allocated to which user. With user management system, the administrator can manage user privileges based on their role in institution. Admin can create a new user, provide a password and grant and revoke their access. According to the role, an individual can access the fields and view their responsibilities.

### What all you can do

- Search for any users using the search bar.
- View profile of all users based on the roles.
- View/Edit users password and privileges.
- Set privileges to users as per their roles.
- Allows organization to set standards for different roles based on their responsibility.

## Upcoming Features

### Gradebook Module Update

**Skills based subject evaluation mode**

It has been observed, especially in lower class that there are many institutions that grade their learners based on the skills they have acquired in their curriculum. With this update, institutes can assess an individual student's performance & progress on each subject based on the skills they acquired during the learning process. An example:

How much proficient a student is in:

- Reading in English?
- Writing in English?

**Subject groups based evaluation**

Ability to club the different subjects under one section, for example Physics, Chemistry & Biology can be grouped together under "Science" while creating examination report card.

**Attendance in Report Card**

A new filter will be available in the gradebook tab i.e. attendance. With this option, institutes will be able to showcase the attendance data in a learner's report card.

The attendance data can be directly fetched from attendance module (also termed as automated biometric data) but if the user wants to enter the attendance manually along with the process of entering score, in that case an interface can be used.

**Remarks in Gradebook**

There is now an ability to add the remarks in the gradebook. With this setting, institutes can customize their remark setting and create the remarks either subject-wise or other curriculum activities wise while creating a report.

### Finance Module Update

**Improving the 'Pay All Fees' page**

'Pay All Fees' is a single page to manage all types of fee transactions. This year we are planning to reform the Pay All Fees Page so that institutes can add the discounts, other offers and fine option on the fee page.

**Common fee particulars**

Particulars can be created once and reused to allow proper reporting and fee head-wise reports.

### Transport Module

**GPS tracking**

While the students commute from school to home, the security is a huge aspect for both parents & the institution. With this new feature school & parents can both track the route, location & stops of the buses in real time straight from the Mobile App.

**Discount & Fine in Transport Fee**

Ability to give discounts & apply fines in the transport fee.
