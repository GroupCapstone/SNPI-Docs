School management software
A tool which automates the institute’s daily
operations and facilitates paperless
administration for the schools, resulting in
better growth and higher productivity.

In this digital era, it is vital and requisite that

every institution should have modern and

reliable school management system, so that

they can make better and faster decisions to

maintain the growth of the alliance. But

sometimes due to unawareness of the

important features of school software, school

might end up using a software which fails to

fulfill their needs.

management software feature glossary

Upcoming Features

GRADEBOOK MODULE UPDATE
Skills based subject evaluation mode
It has been observed, especially in lower class that there are many institu-
tions that grade their learners based on the skills they have acquired in their
curriculum. With this update, institutes can assess an individual student’s
performance & progress on each subject based on the skills they acquired
during the learning process. An example:

How much proficient a student is in :
• Reading in English?
• Writing in English?

Subject groups based evaluation
Ability to club the different subjects under one section, for example Physics,
Chemistry & Biology can be grouped together under “Science” while creating
examination report card.

Attendance in Report Card
A new filter will be available in the gradebook tab i.e. attendance. With this
option, institutes will be able to showcase the attendance data in a learner’s
report card.

The attendance data can be directly fetched from attendance module (also
termed as automated biometric data) but if the user wants to enter the atten-
dance manually along with the process of entering score, in that case an
interface can be used.

Remarks in Gradebook
There is now an ability to add the remarks in the gradebook. With this
setting, institutes can customize their remark setting and create the remarks
either subject-wise or other curriculum activities wise while creating a report

FINANCE MODULE UPDATE
Improving the ‘Pay All Fees’ page
‘Pay All Fees’ is a single page to manage all types of fee transactions. This year
we are planning to reform the Pay All Fees Page so that institutes can add the
discounts, other offers and fine option on the fee page.

Common fee particulars
Particulars can be created once and reused to allow proper reporting and fee
head-wise reports

TRANSPORT MODULE
GPS tracking
While the students commute from school to home, the security is a huge
aspect for both parents & the institution. With this new feature school & par-
ents can both track the route, location & stops of the buses in real time
straight from the Fedena Mobile App.

Discount & Fine in Transport Fee
Ability to give discounts & apply fines in the transport fee.
