The 4M Music Check

Mood matches what you're filming

Instrumental track used under any voiceover

Vocals used only when there's no talking

Music volume is 20–30% of voice volume

Tested on phone speakers, not headphones

Music starts on frame one, with no silent opening

Track runs underneath every cut with no dead air

No mid-video track swap unless it's a deliberate beat drop
