Form error messages
Your error message gets ignored. So raise fewer of them: accept spaces and hyphens, and wait until someone has finished a field before you flag it. When they hit submit, mark each error right at its field, and add a linked summary on top. Say what went wrong and how to fix it, and keep what they typed. And while the form is submitting, disable the button. That's how your error gets read, not ignored.
