2 . THEATER BUILDING
Location Kampong Cham, Province
Type Academic | Individual
Year Undergraduate 3rd Year
This theater building is envisioned as a modern cultural landmark that celebrates local heritage while providing a world-class venue for performing arts, cultural events, and community gatherings. Its architectural design draws inspiration from traditional Khmer roof forms and decorative motifs, reinterpreted through contemporary materials such as glass, steel, and concrete to create a welcoming and iconic civic space.
The transparent façade symbolizes openness, creativity, and connection, allowing visitors to experience the vibrant activities within while inviting the community to engage with the arts. The dramatic entrance and soaring rooflines create a memorable identity, expressing dignity, cultural pride, and artistic excellence.

1 . MIX-USED DEVELOPMENT
Location Boeung kak , Phnom Penh
Type Academic | Individual
Year Undergraduate 4th Year
mixed-use development is envisioned as a landmark of modern urban living, integrating residential, commercial, hospitality, office, and recreational functions into a single, sustainable destination. The project is designed to create a vibrant environment where people can live, work, shop, and socialize, reducing travel time while enhancing convenience and quality of life.
The twin towers, connected by sky bridges, symbolize connectivity, innovation, and collaboration, reflecting the dynamic relationship between people, businesses, and the surrounding community. The flowing architectural form and vertical greenery create a distinctive identity while promoting environmental sustainability, wellness, and harmony with nature.

Form:
The building form is derived from a simple square geometric shape, which serves as the foundation for the overall architectural composition.
Architectural Style:
The design is inspired by the abstraction and transformation of the traditional Khmer Phka Chan (Moon Flower) motif. Although the building expresses a contemporary architectural language, its core concept is rooted in the essence of Khmer decorative art, creating a harmonious balance between tradition and modernity.
Identity:
The project reflects a distinct Khmer identity by integrating traditional cultural values into a modern architectural expression.
Spatial Design:
The spatial planning is developed through a comprehensive environmental analysis, taking into account orientation, prevailing wind, solar heat, natural daylight, and the surrounding landscape to create comfortable, sustainable, and context-responsive spaces.

Boeung Kak City, commonly referred to as the Boeung Kak Satellite City Development Project, is a prominent and large-scale urban redevelopment area located in the heart of Phnom Penh, Cambodia. The development covers approximately 114 hectares, extending from Srah Chak Sangkat in Daun Penh Khan into Tuol Kork Khan. It has transformed the area from one of Phnom Penh's largest natural lakes into a carefully planned commercial and residential district. Today, the area features modern condominium buildings, corporate offices, international schools, and various facilities for sports and recreational activities.
This project aims to restore the natural environment in order to preserve the identity of the area while transforming it into a modern commercial district that looks toward an advanced future. The project demonstrates how an area can be developed by integrating nature and local identity with modern technology. Development does not necessarily mean eliminating the existing identity; instead, it can create a balance between nature, culture, identity, and technological advancement.
