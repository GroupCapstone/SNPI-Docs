Priming
There's an empty field and suddenly you want a coupon. That field can pull a ready buyer out of checkout. A coupon field implies a discount before it's confirmed. Shoppers without a code feel they overpay. Hide the field and reveal it only on request. This is called priming. A signal changes later behavior, without the person noticing the cause.
